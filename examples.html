Starting out

Saving well, about $4,200 a year, but all of it sitting in a savings account earning 1.5%.

  2. Kept $10,000 aside as her emergency fund first
  3. Moved $4,200 a year into a structured plan and added protection

Planning for retirement

50, retiring at 60, money earning about 2%

Had $300,000 in his own investments, earning about 2% a year.

  1. Worked out what his $300,000 was really earning him
  2. Restructured it into a 10-year plan around his retirement date
  3. Showed him the numbers year by year so he could check them himself
  4. He could see where every dollar went before he said yes

Common situations

Planning for retirement

50, retiring at 60, money earning about 2%

  1. Worked out what his $300,000 was really earning him

  3. Showed him the numbers year by year so he could check them himself
  4. He could see where every dollar went before he said yes

How these figures are worked out: simple illustrations assuming contributions at the start of each year and returns compounded yearly, before fees and charges.
